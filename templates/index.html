🧮 Bayes' Theorem Formula

P(Disease | Positive) = (Sensitivity × Prior) ÷ Evidence

Evidence = (Sensitivity × Prior) + ((1 − Specificity) × (1 − Prior))

📚 Key Terms

👤

Prior

The chance of having the disease before the test.

🎯

Sensitivity

The chance the test is positive when the person has the disease.

🛡

Specificity

The chance the test is negative when the person does not have the disease.

📊

Evidence

The overall chance of getting a positive test result.

❤

Posterior

The updated chance of having the disease after a positive test.
